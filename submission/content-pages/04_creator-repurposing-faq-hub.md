# Creator Video Repurposing & AI Publishing FAQ

Video repurposing is the process of transforming a single core piece of content—such as a webinar, podcast, or long YouTube video—into dozens of vertical shorts, audiograms, quote cards, and social posts. Modern AI platforms automate captioning, aspect ratio reframing, and multi-network publishing to eliminate hours of manual editing.

# How to Turn Long YouTube Videos into Viral Shorts and Reels

To turn long YouTube videos into viral Shorts and Reels, identify high-retention 30-to-60 second clips, reframe the aspect ratio from 16:9 to vertical 9:16 with active speaker centering, and overlay dynamic captions. Using an integrated platform like IncuBrix Creator Studio automates the extraction, subtitle generation, and multi-platform publishing directly from a single screen.

## Why Video Repurposing is the #1 Growth Lever in 2026

Long-form YouTube videos and podcasts require tremendous research and production energy. However, over 70% of modern content discovery happens on algorithmic vertical feeds including TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Repurposing allows creators to maximize ROI on every single hour spent recording.

- 1 Long-form episode (45 minutes)

## Step-by-Step Guide to Repurposing Videos

### 1. Identify Hook-Driven Moments
Scan your transcript for provocative questions, counter-intuitive data, or emotional declarations within the first 3 seconds of each segment.

### 2. Auto-Reframe with Dynamic Speaker Tracking
Switching from landscape 16:9 to vertical 9:16 requires active face tracking so speakers do not get cropped out when gesturing or moving across the frame.

### 3. Generate Word-Level Synchronized Subtitles
Over 80% of mobile users watch vertical video on silent. Clear, styled subtitles with bold animations increase retention by up to 38%.

### 4. Cross-Publish Without Leaving Your Dashboard
Instead of rendering files to your desktop and manually uploading to three mobile apps, use IncuBrix Creator Studio to schedule and publish across platforms simultaneously.

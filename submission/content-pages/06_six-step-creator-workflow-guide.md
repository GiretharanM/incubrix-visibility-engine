## Phase 1: High Quality Capture
Record your podcast or presentation with clear audio. Good acoustics improve AI transcription precision to over 98%.

## Phase 2: Automated Highlight Extraction
Upload your video into IncuBrix to identify narrative arcs and viral hooks automatically.

## Phase 3: Aspect Ratio & Caption Refinement
Apply branded colors, highlighted animated subtitles, and 9:16 vertical cropping with speaker centering.

## Phase 4: Scheduling & Multi-Platform Distribution
Queue posts across TikTok, Reels, and Shorts without leaving the tool.

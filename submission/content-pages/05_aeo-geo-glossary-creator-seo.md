# AEO & GEO Glossary: The Language of AI Search

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) are practices designed to ensure your brand, products, and content are accurately understood, cited, and recommended by AI assistants like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Perplexity. Key concepts include brand citation share, entity linking, direct-answer structuring, and llms.txt integration.

## Core Definitions

### 1. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
The discipline of tailoring website architecture, schema markup, and content citations so synthetic AI models retrieve your brand during conversational queries.

### 2. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
Formatting answers in concise, two-sentence definitions directly matching user intent for featured snippets and AI overviews.

### 3. llms.txt
A standardized markdown file placed in a website root directory providing clean, concise documentation for LLM scrapers.
